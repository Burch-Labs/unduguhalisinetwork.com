---

## DELIVERY TIPS

1. **Speak naturally** - Don't read robotically. Use your hotel sales experience tone (confident, relationship-focused)
2. **Pause for clicks** - Pause 1-2 seconds when showing portal actions so viewers can see what's happening
3. **Emphasize autonomy** - Stress "automatically," "agent handles it," "without me"
4. **Show the proof** - Let the portal do the talking (successful booking creation, countdown timer, calendar update)
5. **End strong** - That "Prove me wrong" closing is powerful. Own it.
