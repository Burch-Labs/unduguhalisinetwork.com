📜 Terms & Conditions (Rack & STO)

Lilita Keper Luxury Safari Lodge - Booking Terms

1. RACK RATES vs STO RATES

Rack rates are the published full rates. STO (Straight Ticketable Office) rates offer a 20% discount on all rack rates listed above, applicable for travel agents booking directly.

2. BOOKING CONFIRMATION

All bookings must be confirmed with a 30% deposit within 7 days. Final balance due 60 days before arrival.

3. CANCELLATION POLICY

• Cancellation 90+ days before arrival: Full refund minus $100 administrative fee
• Cancellation 60-89 days: 50% refund
• Cancellation 30-59 days: 25% refund
• Cancellation less than 30 days: No refund

4. INCLUSIONS (All-Inclusive Package)

• Accommodation in luxury tent/villa
• All meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
• Selected premium beverages
• Game drives and wildlife activities
• Airport transfers
• WiFi access

5. EXCLUSIONS

• International flights
• Travel insurance (strongly recommended)
• Personal expenses and tips
• Additional activities beyond standard offerings

6. SPECIAL REQUESTS

Special dietary requirements, celebrations, or custom arrangements should be noted at booking and are subject to confirmation.

7. MINIMUM STAY

Peak season bookings require minimum 3-night stay. Off-season minimum 2 nights available.

8. GROUP BOOKINGS

Groups of 8+ may qualify for additional discounts. Contact sales for quote.

9. STO 20% DISCOUNT TERMS

STO discount applies to published rack rates only. Not valid with other promotions. Valid for bookings made through registered travel agents. Proof of agent credentials may be required.

10. FORCE MAJEURE

In case of unforeseen circumstances (natural disasters, pandemics, etc.), we will work with guests on alternative dates or provide full refunds.

For questions about rates or terms, contact our reservations team or your travel agent.
